## What I Do

### Software Engineering

I have been developing software since the early 2000s, working across different programming languages, technologies, and development environments.

My experience includes frontend engineering, application architecture, API integrations, automation, testing, localization, and independent software development.

I enjoy understanding complex systems, exploring architectural approaches, and building solutions that are practical, maintainable, and reusable.

### Open Source & Developer Tools

I develop and maintain open-source projects, create developer utilities, and experiment with new approaches to software development.

I believe in sharing knowledge and building tools that other people can inspect, use, adapt, and improve.

My work spans PHP, JavaScript, frontend technologies, content management systems, and various development tools.

### Education & Knowledge Sharing

I create programming courses, educational publications, practical exercises, and learning resources.

Through my teaching work on Stepik, I help students understand programming concepts and develop practical skills across multiple technologies, including SQL, JavaScript, Python, PHP, Go, Kotlin, Java, and C#.

I believe that good education should go beyond memorizing syntax. It should help people understand how things work and become independent problem-solvers.

### Photography & Visual Storytelling

Photography is another way I explore the world.

I work with street, travel, portrait, and documentary photography, capturing people, places, architecture, and everyday moments.

My personal photographic projects explore human experiences, identity, belonging, distance, memory, and the relationship between people and the places they inhabit.

### Independent Projects

I enjoy building things independently, experimenting with ideas, and turning interesting problems into working products.

My projects include digital tools, educational platforms, publications, photography-related utilities, and logic games.

I value curiosity, creative freedom, simplicity, and the opportunity to take an idea from its first experiment to a finished product.
